Traktor Bridge

Traktor Bridge was designed for users of Traktor, the DJ software from Native Instruments. It is a central hub that
lets the leading DJ programs exchange playlists. It works alongside Traktor and does not replace it.

any noncommercial use (PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, see LICENSE.txt in the install
folder). The source code is public: everyone is free to read it.

of data (a collection, a USB key or its content, music files, cues, playlists), for a key a
player refuses to read, or for any other damage resulting from its use. Formatting a key erases
it. Back up your collection and test a key on your player before playing out with it.
